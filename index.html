Getting to know PeraFlow.

Is PeraFlow free to use?

Yes. Core finance tracking is free, including income, expenses, accounts, transfers, categories, opening balances, and local backup and restore. Free use includes ads and active account limits. Premium is optional.

Do I need an account to get started?

You don’t need a PeraFlow account for local finance tracking. Google authorization is needed only if you choose to use Google Drive backup and restore.

Is PeraFlow connected to my bank?

PeraFlow is a personal cashbook. You record your transactions and transfers yourself. Accounts in the app help organize your records; they don’t connect to your bank or move real money.

How can I back up or restore my data?

Create a local backup, or choose optional Google Drive backup and restore. Free users can access Drive backup through rewarded ads. Premium includes automatic Google Drive backup when enabled. Keep a backup before changing or resetting your device.

What does Premium add?

Premium adds unlimited active accounts, full reports, automatic Google Drive backup, and no ads. Monthly and lifetime purchase options use localized pricing from Google Play, shown in the app.

Where can I download PeraFlow?

PeraFlow is coming to Google Play for Android. It isn’t publicly available yet. The download link will appear here once the public listing is ready.
