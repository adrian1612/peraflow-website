FAQ

Quick answers.

Is PeraFlow free?

Yes. Core money tracking remains available for free. Premium adds unlimited accounts, full reports, automatic Google Drive backup, and removes ads.

Does PeraFlow require an account?

PeraFlow is designed around local personal finance data. Google authorization is only needed when you choose Google Drive backup features.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. PeraFlow supports local backup and restore, plus optional Google Drive backup and restore.
